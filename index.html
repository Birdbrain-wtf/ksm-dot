Kusama and Polkadot

KSM and DOT, before and after the cap

On 14 March 2026 Polkadot cut new DOT by more than half and capped the supply at 2.1 billion. Kusama kept issuing at about 10% a year. If issuance drives price, KSM should have lost ground to DOT since then. Here is what the prices say.

"Over the last couple of years, KSM has traded highly correlated with DOT's price against other tokens & fiat. And 8 months of data isn't a lot, but KSM/DOT is doing better than it was before DOT switched to fixed supply cap model. … I'm at least comfortable to claim that KSM held its ground relative to DOT."

Lederstrumpf, Polkadot forum, 13 November 2026

What each chain is issuing now

Measured from each chain's total supply on Asset Hub, 18 May to 13 November 2026, then annualised.

Kusama

10.5% a year

About 1.87 million new KSM a year on a supply of 19.1 million. No cap. Holders can change this by vote.

Polkadot

3.4% a year

About 56 million new DOT a year on 1.71 billion, down from 120 million before 14 March 2026. Capped at 2.1 billion, stepping down every two years.

So since March, new KSM has arrived about three times as fast as new DOT, relative to supply. If that alone set the price, the KSM/DOT line above would be falling.

What this shows and what it does not

  • Prices are one daily close in US dollars. KSM in DOT is the KSM price divided by the DOT price. Dividing removes whatever moves the whole market, which is why it is the fair test.
  • "How closely they move" is the correlation of daily price changes over the last 90 days: 1 means they always move together, 0 means no relationship.
  • Eight months is short. Prices answer to many things at once, so this cannot prove that issuance does not matter. It can show whether the effect people predicted is visible yet.
  • Where you start counting changes the answer. Measured from the day the cap took effect, KSM has gained on DOT. Measured against the year before the cap was proposed, KSM buys fewer DOT than it did. Both are on this page.
  • The issuance figures are a fixed reading, not live. Everything else updates once a day.
